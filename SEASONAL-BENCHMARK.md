Phuket’s NASA POWER grid record contains 537 wet December days and 316 wet January days out of 930 each. The 1 mm threshold measures how often any qualifying rain fell during the whole day. It cannot distinguish a brief shower from a lost beach afternoon. The same limitation applies to the new island and coastal bases.

Aruba represents the Caribbean example. Barbados and Curaçao do not have catalog entries. Sydney and Perth are scored separately, as are Cambodia and southern Vietnam, and Oman and the UAE. Galápagos contributes one result even when all three bases qualify.

Reproduce from the repository root with the installed build tools.

```sh
./node_modules/.bin/esbuild scripts/compare-seasonal.ts --bundle --platform=node --outfile=/tmp/compare-seasonal.cjs
node /tmp/compare-seasonal.cjs
```
